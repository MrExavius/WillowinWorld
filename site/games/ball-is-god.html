Ball is God?!

Hardcore vertical descent arcade about timing and fate.

Ball is God?! is a fast mobile arcade game about guiding Espa down a divine tower, slipping through gaps between broken olympic stones and surviving one-touch decisions with story consequences.

Game overview

A tense vertical descent about one-touch timing, myth and consequences.

Player promise

Guide Espa down a divine tower, slip through broken stone gaps and survive runs where one mistake can end everything.

Core loop

Read moving platforms, time the drop, avoid impact, face boss-like pressure and make story choices that affect the world.

Audience

Arcade players who enjoy hard timing, one-touch control, mythic visuals, boss pressure and story-flavored challenge.

Status

Development pass: descent readability, cloud depth, stone silhouettes and story beats are being refined.

Publisher angle

A one-touch arcade hook with a stronger world than a score chaser.

Ball is God?! combines a simple vertical timing mechanic with divine tower imagery, character stakes and moral-choice framing. The goal is a readable arcade game that still feels like a strange authored world.
