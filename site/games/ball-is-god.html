Gameplay loop

Read the fall, time the gap and survive the divine tower.

  • Step 01Read the descent

    The player scans the next platforms, gaps, hazards and safe timing windows while the world pushes downward.

  • Step 02Rotate the tower

    Horizontal drag turns the world around Espa, aligning a safe platform or a deliberate gap.

  • Step 03Risk and smash

    Skip three platforms to charge a combo smash, then break through structures while the shield covers one lethal mistake.

  • Step 04Choose the consequence

    Mercy and force shape karma, dialogue and the gameplay rules carried into enemies and bosses.

Story / world

A divine tower, a falling hero and a mythic world built for consequences.

Ball is God?! began from a childhood fascination with mobile vertical-descent arcades: reading gaps, reacting quickly and surviving by instinct.

But the format also felt like it could carry much more - bosses, personality, story, moral tension and a world that feels authored rather than disposable. The current project follows Espa through ten location scenes, strange characters and choices that change both story and play.

That became the question behind Ball is God?!: what if a simple vertical descent arcade had the pressure of a boss game and the emotional consequences of a story-driven RPG?

Development journey

From first studio idea to larger narrative arcade project.

  • July 2024Original concept

    Ball is God?! began as WillowinWorld's first game idea: a fast vertical arcade game about falling through a divine tower and surviving stone gaps.

  • August 2024Story direction begins

    After the first internal cut, the team decided the game should become more than a simple arcade descent.

  • Following monthsWorld and boss expansion

    The project grew into ten location scenes, boss encounters, moral choices and a more authored mythic structure.

  • April-May 2025Story rewrite

    The story was rewritten and expanded after months of drafting, improving character abilities, world logic and moral consequences.

  • February 2026Project scope review

    The team reviewed the full scale of the project after story integration and recognized it had become a larger narrative arcade game.

  • July 2026Active systems, UI and world pass

    Production now spans ten location scenes, deterministic tower generation, combo smash, shield, karma-driven rules, level-selection UI and expression-ready dialogue portraits.

Ball is God?! FAQ

Answers for players, press and publishing partners.

What kind of game is Ball is God?!

A hardcore vertical descent arcade about rotating a tower around Espa, risking safe landings and breaking through mythic worlds.

How does the gameplay work?

Drag horizontally to rotate the tower, skip three platforms to charge a smash, and swipe vertically when the cooldown shield must absorb a lethal hit.

What makes it different?

It connects risk-reward vertical arcade pressure with ten locations and karma that changes dialogue, enemies, telegraphs, shields and bosses.

What is the current status?

Active production across runtime systems, world art, narrative content, mobile feel and production validation.

Can public assets be used?

Yes, for press, publisher review and store references. Source assets stay private unless requested directly.
