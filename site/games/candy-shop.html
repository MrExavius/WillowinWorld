Game overview

Candy Shop is a cozy candy merge puzzle with soft physics.

The idea

Drop sweets, build bigger treats and let soft physics turn every run into a chain of merges.

Current build

Ten container rulesets, a complete 13-candy chain, eight special effects, landing preview, hold and a next-three queue are implemented.

Audience

Casual puzzle players who enjoy drop-and-merge games, cozy candy visuals, low-pressure strategy and quick mobile sessions.

Status

Active development. We are working on mobile performance, UI polish, candy balance, container difficulty and clearer combo feedback.

Features

What's in Candy Shop.

Drop-and-merge candy play

You place sweets into the container, use gravity and timing, then merge matching candies into larger treats.

13-stage candy chain

The chain gives every run a goal you can see: merge your way from the smallest sweet to the biggest candy.

Combos and special sweets

Eight special-candy effects combine into paired recipes and side effects, so repeat runs play differently.

Soft candy-shop look

Soft colors, candy-shop backgrounds and a touch-friendly interface for short sessions on a phone.

Gameplay loop

How to play Candy Shop

  • Step 01Read the next candy

    Check the next sweet, the current stack and the space left in the container.

  • Step 02Choose where to drop

    A drop can set up a clean merge, block a later move or build pressure on the stack.

  • Step 03Merge and combo

    Matching candies merge into bigger treats, and good placement sets off chain reactions.

  • Step 04Progress and repeat

    Chase higher candy stages, better runs and bigger combos.

Story / world

A candy shop where small sweets grow into big ones.

Candy Shop began in 2025 as our second game idea: a bright, cozy candy merge puzzle with soft, satisfying physics.

The setting is a candy shop: shelves, sweets, soft mascots and shop-like containers. Every tiny candy can grow into a bigger treat.

After the first prototype we expanded the design document, reworked the direction, finished the 13-candy merge chain and built ten container rulesets, special recipes, planning tools and the first progression systems.

Candy Shop FAQ

Common questions about Candy Shop

What kind of game is Candy Shop?

A candy merge puzzle with soft physics. You drop candies into a container and merge matching ones into bigger candies, much like in Suika Game, with special sweets and combos on top.

How do you play Candy Shop?

Choose where to drop the next candy, merge matching sweets, manage the space in the container and work up the 13-stage chain.

Is Candy Shop out yet?

Not yet. It is in active development, and the current project has the merge chain, ten container rulesets, special recipes, a daily challenge, the Candy Book and local score tables. There is no public release date. The target platforms are iOS and Android.

What makes Candy Shop different?

Ten containers, each with its own shape and rules, change how a familiar merge loop plays. Special sweets add recipes and combos.

Who makes Candy Shop?

WillowinWorlds, an independent studio making mobile puzzle and arcade games.

Can I use Candy Shop press assets?

Yes, for press, publisher review and store references. Source files stay private unless you ask us for them.
