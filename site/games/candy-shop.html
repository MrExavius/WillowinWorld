Origin

A bright candy idea rebuilt until the merge loop felt right.

Candy Shop began in 2025 as WillowinWorld's second game idea: a bright, cozy candy merge puzzle built around simple joy and satisfying physics.

After the first prototype, the team expanded the GDD, rebuilt the direction, implemented the core candy mechanics, completed the 13-candy merge chain, started container systems and created the first UI pass.

Publisher angle

A recognizable merge format with a sweeter studio identity.

Candy Shop uses the instantly readable appeal of drop-and-merge puzzle games, then adds a stronger candy identity through irregular shapes, special sweets, container variety and cozy long-term progression.

The game is designed to be understandable in seconds, satisfying in short sessions and expandable through events, collections, cosmetics and long-term content.

Development history

From first prototype to internal beta.

  • Late June 2025Second game idea

    WillowinWorld started exploring a bright candy project built around something simple, joyful and widely appealing.

  • August 2025First candy characters

    The first candy characters were created, and the merge chain later expanded from 11 candies to 13 for stronger progression.

  • September 2025First prototype

    The first prototype was built and tested. The result showed that the idea needed more identity, progression and a clearer hook.

  • October 2025GDD expansion

    The design document was expanded with new mechanics, special sweets, progression ideas and container direction.

  • December 2025-February 2026Core candy systems

    The main candy mechanics were implemented, the candy set was completed and early container systems began.

  • April 2026-currentUI pass and internal beta

    The first major UI elements were created, and the game moved into internal testing, optimization, balance and polish.
