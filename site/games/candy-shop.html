Features

What players do in Candy Shop.

Drop-and-merge candy play

Players place sweets into the container, use gravity and timing, then merge matching candies into larger treats.

13-stage candy chain

The merge ladder gives every run a visible goal, from tiny sweets to bigger reward pieces with stronger screen presence.

Combos and special sweets

Eight implemented special-candy effects form paired recipes and side effects, giving repeated sessions more tactical texture.

Cozy mobile presentation

Soft colors, candy-shop backgrounds and touch-friendly UI make the game readable for short iOS and Android sessions.

Gameplay loop

Choose the drop, control the container and keep the merge chain alive.

  • Step 01Read the next candy

    The player checks the next sweet, the current stack and the available space inside the container.

  • Step 02Place with intention

    A drop can set up a clean merge, block a future move or create pressure that changes the next decision.

  • Step 03Merge and combo

    Matching candies evolve into bigger treats, and chain reactions reward smart placement.

  • Step 04Progress and repeat

    Players chase higher candy stages, better runs, cleaner combos and future progression rewards.

Story / world

A cheerful candy shop where every sweet can become something bigger.

Candy Shop began in 2025 as WillowinWorld's second game idea: a bright, cozy candy merge puzzle built around simple joy and satisfying physics.

The world is intentionally warm and readable: shelves, sweets, soft mascots and shop-like containers support the fantasy that every tiny candy has a path toward becoming a bigger treat.

After the first prototype, the team expanded the GDD, rebuilt the direction, completed the 13-candy merge chain and implemented ten container rulesets, special recipes, planning tools and the first meta-progression systems.

Development journey

From first prototype to a deeper production build.

  • Late June 2025Second game idea

    WillowinWorld started exploring a bright candy project built around something simple, joyful and widely appealing.

  • August 2025First candy characters

    The first candy characters were created, and the merge chain later expanded from 11 candies to 13 for stronger progression.

  • September 2025First prototype

    The first prototype was built and tested. The result showed that the idea needed more identity, progression and a clearer hook.

  • October 2025GDD expansion

    The design document was expanded with new mechanics, special sweets, progression ideas and container direction.

  • December 2025-February 2026Core candy systems

    The main candy mechanics were implemented, the candy set was completed and early container systems began.

  • April-July 2026Systems and polish pass

    The build expanded with ten container rulesets, special-candy interactions, planning tools, daily challenges and collection systems alongside optimization and UI polish.

Candy Shop FAQ

Answers for players, press and publishing partners.

What kind of game is Candy Shop?

A cozy candy merge puzzle with soft physics drops, special sweets, combos and progression.

How does the gameplay work?

Choose a candy, drop it into the container, merge matching sweets, manage space and chase larger candy stages.

What makes it different?

It combines a familiar merge puzzle loop with cozy candy identity, special sweets, readable combos and container pressure.

What is the current status?

Active development. The merge chain, ten container rulesets, special recipes, daily challenge, Candy Book and local score tables exist in the current project.

Can public assets be used?

Yes, for press, publisher review and store references. Source assets stay private unless requested directly.
