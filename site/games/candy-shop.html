Candy Shop

Cozy candy merge puzzle with satisfying physics.

Candy Shop is a casual mobile merge puzzle where players drop and combine sweets into larger treats. It focuses on readable candy physics, combos, special sweets and long-term progression without pay-to-win pressure.

Gameplay pillars

  • Drop and merge sweets through a clear evolution chain.

Game overview

A soft merge loop with readable physics and long-term candy growth.

Player promise

Drop sweets, merge them into bigger treats and manage a cheerful container that gets more satisfying as combos appear.

Core loop

Choose a drop, place it carefully, trigger merges, create special sweets and push the board toward higher-value candy chains.

Audience

Casual puzzle players who enjoy Suika-like merging, soft visuals, low-pressure strategy and quick mobile sessions.

Status

Core loop and progression direction are being refined around candy sizes, container pressure and combo readability.

Publisher angle

A recognizable merge format with a sweeter studio identity.

Candy Shop starts from an instantly readable drop-and-merge loop, then leans into cozy candy art, special sweets, container variety and long-term collection pressure. It is designed to be understandable in seconds but expandable across many sessions.
