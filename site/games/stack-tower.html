Stack Tower

Time the drop. Build the tower.

An early one-tap tower stacker. A crane swings each floor, you pick the moment to drop it, and the landing sets your score, stability and streak.

One scene with the crane, floor drops, landing grades, stability and a HUD is built around a single 20-floor building; menus, audio and progression are not built.

Gameplay loop

How it plays

  1. Step 01Watch the crane

    A crane swings the next floor on a pendulum. It gets faster as the tower grows.

  2. Step 02Drop at the right moment

    One tap, click or press of Space releases the floor, and it falls onto the tower.

  3. Step 03Read the landing

    Each landing is graded Perfect, Good, Risky, Bad or Miss, and the grade moves your stability, score and streak.

Features

What's in the build

Swinging crane

A pendulum crane carries each floor. Speed, swing and drop height rise with every floor up to a cap.

Five landing grades

Perfect, Good, Risky, Bad and Miss depend on how far a floor lands off-centre and how much it overlaps the one below, and each grade scores differently.

Stability and lean

Bad landings drain stability and make the tower lean, and good or perfect ones win it back. The first building starts at 92 stability with two lives.

Six floor types

Normal, Safe, Precision, Heavy, Technical and Golden floors differ in weight, swing speed, score value and stability effects.

Rescue and bonus floors

A Technical floor is offered every sixth floor, or sooner when stability runs low. Five Perfect landings in a row queue a Golden floor.

A 20-floor first building

The Residential Tower targets 20 floors. A run ends on a completed build, a collapse or when the lives run out.

Stack Tower FAQ

Questions and answers

What kind of game is Stack Tower?

A 2D one-tap arcade game. A crane swings each floor over the tower, you drop it, and the landing is graded. It shares the tap-to-drop stacking idea with the studio's Burger Stack and Sandwich Stack; this one is a side view with a crane, floor types and a stability meter.

Which platforms, and what is the status?

It is an early prototype designed for mobile in portrait orientation. It is built as one scene with one building, and there is no public build, store page or release date yet.
