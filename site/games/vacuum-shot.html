Vacuum Shot

Vacuum a prop. Fire it. Fly.

A vertical slice of an endless recoil-flight game. Tap a prop to pull it into the bag, fire it for recoil, then land softly to keep your score chain going.

One vacuum, 39 props, an endless route and an upgrade panel are in the Unity project with automated tests; audio and device builds are still to come.

Gameplay loop

How it plays

  1. Step 01Vacuum a prop

    Tap a prop in the meadow or the sky to pull it into the bag. The bag holds one item, and its power rating shows what a shot will do.

  2. Step 02Fire and fly

    Tap anywhere else to fire the item at fixed strength. The recoil launches the pilot forward and up, with one second between shots and two air corrections per flight.

  3. Step 03Land softly

    A stable landing refills your air shots and scores by grade. Chaining controlled landings raises the bonus.

Features

What's in the build

Vacuum and fire

Where BlastBlast! pumps and releases an extinguisher, Vacuum Shot picks a prop and fires it at fixed strength, so the choice of prop is the decision.

39 props in five roles

Air correction, balanced, soft landing, forward dash and vertical lift props each show a POWER 1/5 to 5/5 rating. Ten of them float in the air until collected.

Graded landings

A landing needs about a quarter second of stable contact. It is graded Perfect, Safe or Rough, and consecutive controlled landings build a chain of up to eight.

Endless garden route

Rolling hills and valleys with no blocking obstacles make up the route, and the garden fades into a high-altitude sky when you fly high.

Motor and Bag upgrades

Two upgrade branches, Motor and Bag, go up to level 4 and are paid for with Dew. Progress is saved separately for each vacuum.

Tested rules

Launch, landing, camera and progression rules have EditMode tests, and scene and physics behaviour has PlayMode tests.

Vacuum Shot FAQ

Questions and answers

What kind of game is Vacuum Shot?

An endless recoil-flight arcade game. You vacuum up props, fire them to launch your pilot forward, and land softly to build a score chain. It is built on the same base as the studio's BlastBlast!, with a pastel garden, a vacuum instead of an extinguisher and props that set the power of each shot.

Which platforms, and what is the status?

It is a vertical slice aimed at Android and iOS. Device profiling, final audio and signed builds are still ahead, and there is no public build, store page or release date yet.
