BlastBlast!

Pump. Blast. Ride the recoil.

A one-button endless run. Pump the extinguisher on the pilot's back, blast at the right moment and let the recoil carry you as far as the gas lasts.

The Unity project has one endless run scene, a main menu, a shop and settings. Android and iOS builds and on-device testing are still ahead.

Gameplay loop

How BlastBlast! plays

  1. Step 01Pump

    Tap quickly to build pressure in the extinguisher. Above 100% the tank leaks gas, and at 130% it bursts.

  2. Step 02Blast

    Hold anywhere and release. The nozzle aims itself from the pilot's vertical speed, and the recoil pushes the pilot the opposite way.

  3. Step 03Thread the gates

    Pass each six-gate sector to win back gas and build the score multiplier. The run ends when the gas runs out or the tank bursts.

Features

Current BlastBlast! mechanics and hazards

Recoil flight

Blast timing and power are the only choices in a run. There is no manual aiming: gas leaves along the visible nozzle and the recoil pushes the other way.

Flow Gates

Optional gates in six-gate sectors restore safe gas and build a Flow multiplier. A hard impact or a skipped gate breaks it.

Endless canyon

Terrain comes in 16-meter chunks that join end to end, with bumpers, boost vents, wind corridors and rotors. Difficulty ramps up over the first 640 meters.

Ten extinguishers

New finishes unlock at best distances from 250 to 100,000 meters. Each extinguisher has four upgrade tracks (capacity, efficiency, thrust, stability) up to level 10.

Text-free HUD

Gameplay uses gauges and symbols instead of text: pressure ticks, a six-node route rail, a distance readout and a gas vessel.

Local progress and settings

Progress is saved on the device with backup recovery. Settings cover volume, camera shake, reduced motion, quality and a 60 or 120 FPS target.

Direction

BlastBlast! development stage and roadmap

There is no release date yet. These are goals for the project, not promises.

  • Android and iOS builds, with performance, touch input and app-lifecycle testing on real devices.
  • Authored, licensed sound to replace the audio that is currently created in code.
  • Human playtests of the first run, pump fatigue and the difficulty curve.
  • Accessibility checks for color separation, camera shake and flashing.

Questions and answers

BlastBlast! FAQ

What kind of game is BlastBlast!?

A one-button endless physics runner. Pump pressure into an extinguisher, blast, and use the recoil to travel as far as you can. Vacuum Shot uses the same base with a vacuum and props instead of an extinguisher.

Is BlastBlast! out yet, and which platforms is it for?

Not yet. BlastBlast! is a vertical slice with no public build and no release date. The Unity project is set up for Android and iOS, but builds and testing on devices are still ahead.

Who makes BlastBlast!, and how can I follow it?

BlastBlast! is made by WillowinWorlds, an independent mobile game studio (see its main games). Follow WillowinWorlds on the social channels linked on this page, or email willowinworlds@gmail.com to ask for updates on BlastBlast!.
