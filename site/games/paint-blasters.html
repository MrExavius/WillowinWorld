Players combine color logic and charge behavior before firing, turning each projectile into a small tactical choice.

Read tower weak points

Block shapes, colors and tower structure create clear targets for smart impacts and better chain reactions.

Arcade puzzle pacing

Short mobile levels keep the action readable while leaving room for mastery, replay and future challenge modes.

Gameplay loop

Mix the projectile, aim at the weakness and learn from the collapse.

  • Step 01Read the tower

    The player studies block color, structure, balance and likely weak points before committing to a shot.

  • Step 02Mix the shot

    Color choice and charge type define how the projectile should behave on impact.

  • Step 03Aim and blast

    Touch-friendly aiming turns the plan into a projectile, impact and physics reaction.

  • Step 04Chain or adjust

    A strong hit creates collapse. A partial hit teaches the next color choice, angle or weak point.

Story / world

A bright mechanical arena where color is both ammunition and strategy.

Paint Blasters explores an original twist on mobile physics puzzles: players build each projectile before firing it, mixing color logic with tower destruction and arcade impact.

The world leans into a loud workshop fantasy: pipes, modules, color tanks and mechanical blasters frame each level like an experiment that is about to explode.

The unique strength is not only destruction, but the decision before the shot. Players are not just firing projectiles - they are building them.

Paint Blasters FAQ

Quick answers for players, press, publishers and search.

What kind of game is Paint Blasters?

A mobile color destruction physics puzzle arcade about mixing shots and turning impacts into chain reactions.

How does the gameplay work?

Read the tower, build a color-powered projectile, aim at a weak point, fire and use the collapse to plan the next shot.

What makes it different?

The player does not only shoot. They build the projectile first, mixing color strategy with physics destruction.

What is the current status?

Prototype and production draft. The focus is projectile readability, tower clarity and color-combo logic.

Can public assets be used?

Yes, for press, publisher review and store references. Source assets stay private unless requested directly.
