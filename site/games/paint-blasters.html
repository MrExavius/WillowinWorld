Paint Blasters

Color Destruction Physics Puzzle

Paint Blasters is a vibrant mobile puzzle arcade where players build color-powered projectiles before every shot, aim at readable towers and trigger satisfying chain reactions through physics-based destruction.

The twist is simple: the shot matters before it flies. Players mix colors and charge types to create different effects, then use timing, aim and tower reading to turn one impact into a bigger collapse.

Origin

An original color-combination twist on mobile physics destruction.

Paint Blasters explores an original twist on mobile physics puzzles: players build each projectile before firing it, mixing color logic with tower destruction and arcade impact.

The unique strength is not only destruction, but the decision before the shot. Players are not just firing projectiles - they are building them.

Publisher angle

Clear arcade impact with tactical color decisions.

Paint Blasters combines the immediate appeal of aim-and-shoot physics gameplay with a deeper pre-shot decision system. The result is a mobile-friendly destruction puzzle that is easy to understand in a short clip, but has room for tactical mastery.

The current GDD-based prototype is largely implemented in draft form, while final production scope is still being refined.

Development history
