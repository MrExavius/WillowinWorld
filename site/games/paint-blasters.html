Paint Blasters

Color destruction physics puzzle with arcade impact.

Paint Blasters is a vibrant mobile puzzle arcade where players build color-powered projectiles before every shot, smash towers and trigger satisfying chain reactions through readable physics.

Game overview

A color-powered destruction puzzle where the shot matters before it flies.

Player promise

Build a color-charged projectile, aim at a readable tower and watch the hit turn into chain reactions and arcade destruction.

Core loop

Read block colors, choose a payload, aim for weak points, trigger effects and clear the structure with tactical precision.

Audience

Players who like physics puzzles, aim-and-shoot timing, Match-3 logic, color strategy and punchy mobile feedback.

Status

Production pass: projectile effects, tower readability and chain reaction pacing are being tuned for fast comprehension.

Publisher angle

Clear arcade impact with tactical color decisions.

Paint Blasters is built for short clips: color choice, aim, collision and collapse all happen in one readable moment. The hook is not only destruction, but the decision that happens before the shot.
