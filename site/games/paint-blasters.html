Game overview

A color-mixing physics puzzle about taking down towers.

The idea

Build the shot, hit the weak point and watch one well-placed impact bring down a chain of blocks.

Current build

All 35 projectile recipes, seven structural block archetypes and progressive five-floor tower pressure are implemented in the production prototype.

Audience

Players who like physics puzzles, aim-and-shoot games and knocking towers down in short levels.

Status

Active development - production prototype. The combo system works. Presentation, tower visibility and the final scene scope are still being worked on.

Features

What Paint Blasters lets you do.

Build color-powered shots

You combine three colors before firing, and the recipe you get decides how the projectile behaves and what it is good at.

Read tower weak points

Block shapes, colors and the structure of the tower show you where a hit will do the most damage.

Trigger physics collapse

Shots break the structure and set the tower moving until it comes down.

Short levels

Levels are short, so replaying one to land a better hit is quick.

Gameplay loop

How to play Paint Blasters

  • Step 01Read the tower

    Study the block colors, the structure and the likely weak points before you shoot.

  • Step 02Mix the shot

    Your three colors become a named recipe that sets how the projectile behaves on impact.

  • Step 03Aim and blast

    Aim with your finger and fire. The projectile hits and the physics takes over.

  • Step 04Chain or adjust

    A strong hit brings the tower down. A partial hit shows you what to change: the colors, the angle or the target.

Story / world

A loud workshop where color is the ammunition.

The twist in Paint Blasters is that you build each projectile before you fire it. Color mixing decides what the shot does to the tower.

The setting is a loud workshop with pipes, modules, color tanks and mechanical blasters, and every level looks like an experiment about to explode.

Paint Blasters FAQ

Common questions about Paint Blasters

What kind of game is Paint Blasters?

A color-mixing physics puzzle. You combine three colors into a projectile, aim at a weak point and try to bring a tower down in a chain reaction.

How do you play Paint Blasters?

Study the tower, mix three colors into a projectile, aim at a weak point and fire. Then use the collapse to plan your next shot.

Is Paint Blasters out yet?

Not yet. It is in active development as a production prototype with all 35 projectile recipes implemented. Presentation, tower visibility and scene scope are still being worked on. There is no public release date. The target platforms are iOS and Android.

What makes Paint Blasters different?

You build the projectile before you shoot it, mixing color strategy with physics destruction.

Who makes Paint Blasters?

WillowinWorlds, an independent studio making mobile puzzle and arcade games.

Can I use Paint Blasters press assets?

Yes, for press, publisher review and store references. Source files stay private unless you ask us for them.
