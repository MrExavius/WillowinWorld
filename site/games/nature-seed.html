Nature Seed Draw a path. Bring them together.

A free draw-to-solve physics puzzle you can play in your browser. Draw a line with limited ink and it becomes a ramp, bridge or catch point that carries Water Drop to Seed. Each solved level restores a watercolor forest scene.

Released 18 September 2026 with 100 levels, line-efficiency stars and saved progress. It is free on itch.io, Game Jolt and indiexpo; the iOS and Android version is the target and is not in the app stores yet.

Game overview

A relaxing draw-to-solve puzzle about getting two small characters together.

The idea

Draw a path, bring Water Drop and Seed together and watch the quiet sketch return to life.

Launch build

100 playable levels with touch drawing, stars for line efficiency and progress saved between sessions.

Audience

Players who like calm puzzles, light physics, nature themes and low-stress mobile sessions.

Status

Released on 18 September 2026 and free to play in your browser. A mobile version is the target; it is not in the app stores yet.

Features

What you do in Nature Seed.

Draw-to-solve physical paths

Each puzzle starts with terrain, Seed and Water Drop. The player's stroke becomes the ramp, bridge or catch point that joins them.

Clear physics feedback

Water Drop and Seed move in ways you can follow, so a failed attempt shows you what to change.

Forest restoration rewards

Finish a level and the sketched scene fills with color and growing trees.

Short levels, touch drawing

Levels are short and you draw by touch, which suits quick sessions on a phone.

Gameplay loop

How to play Nature Seed

  • Step 01Read the sketch

    Look for slopes, gaps and blockers, and for the safest way to connect both characters.

  • Step 02Draw the line

    Draw your line. The stroke becomes a ramp, guide or catch point for the characters.

  • Step 03Commit and observe

    Commit the line and watch the characters react. You can see what worked and what did not.

  • Step 04Bloom or refine

    If Water Drop reaches Seed, the forest comes back. If they miss, redraw with what you just learned.

Story / world

A sketched forest waits for water.

Nature Seed started as a draw-to-solve physics puzzle about restoring something fragile rather than pushing objects together.

The cast is Seed, Water Drop and a forest that returns from sketch to color as you play. Each puzzle is a small job: study the land, draw a path and help the water reach something that can grow.

Development timeline

Nature Seed development timeline

  • Summer 2025Original concept

    Nature Seed began as an idea for a calm mobile puzzle about connection and restoring nature.

  • October 2025Seed and Water Drop identity

    We finished the design document and settled on Seed and Water Drop as the main characters.

  • December 2025Development begins

    We planned the first levels on paper, then started building, and the core systems became playable.

  • February 2026Large draft level pass

    A big rough pass over the levels gave us a base for later polish and balancing.

  • March-April 2026Mechanics and level review

    We reviewed extra mechanics and draft levels, then simplified or reworked them to keep the game calm.

  • July 2026Vertical prototype pass

    The July build put limited-ink drawing, efficiency stars, saved progress and stable line generation into one playable loop.

  • 18 September 2026Nature Seed release

    The game was released as Nature Seed with 100 levels, line-efficiency stars, saved progress and watercolor restoration scenes.

Nature Seed FAQ

Common questions about Nature Seed

Is Nature Seed free?

Yes. You can play it free in your browser on itch.io, Game Jolt and indiexpo.

Is Nature Seed out yet?

Yes. It was released on 18 September 2026 with 100 levels. The iOS and Android version is the target platform and is not in the app stores yet.

What kind of game is Nature Seed?

A relaxing draw-to-solve physics puzzle. The lines you draw become solid objects that help Water Drop and Seed meet.

How do you play Nature Seed?

Read the terrain, draw a route with your limited ink, then watch both characters react. If they miss, redraw. Fewer lines earn more stars, and one line earns three.

Who makes Nature Seed?

WillowinWorlds, an independent studio making mobile puzzle and arcade games.

Can I use Nature Seed press assets?

Yes, for press, publisher review and store references. Source files stay private unless you ask us for them.
