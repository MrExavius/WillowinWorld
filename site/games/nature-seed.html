Nature Seed

Relaxing draw-to-solve physics puzzle about restoring nature.

Nature Seed is a meditative mobile physics puzzle where players draw magical lines, guide water toward seeds and turn a dying forest into a living watercolor ecosystem.

Gameplay pillars

  • Draw readable paths that guide water through each puzzle.
  • Restore seeds and transform gray spaces into living nature.
  • Keep every level calm, tactile and mobile-friendly.
Game overview

Built around calm touch input and readable water physics.

Player promise

Solve each level by drawing magical lines, guiding water and watching a quiet forest recover through visible cause and effect.

Core loop

Read the terrain, draw a path, route water to seeds, restore the scene, then unlock the next handcrafted puzzle.

Audience

Players who enjoy relaxing puzzles, eco themes, light physics, cozy restoration and mobile sessions without pressure.

Status

Beta polish: onboarding, level readability and restoration feedback are the current focus before external conversations.

Publisher angle

A cozy puzzle with a clear mobile hook.

Nature Seed has a simple visual pitch: draw a line, move water, revive nature. The mechanic is easy to understand from a short clip, while the restoration fantasy gives the game a softer identity than a generic physics puzzle.
