Nature Seed

Relaxing Draw-to-Solve Physics Puzzle

Nature Seed is a meditative mobile puzzle game about drawing magical lines, guiding water to seeds and restoring a dying forest into a living watercolor world.

What began as a small idea about connection and nature has grown into WillowinWorld's nearest active release. The game is now in beta polish, with the first public beta planned for July 2026.

Gameplay pillars

  • Create simple magical lines with your finger and shape the route water will follow.

Origin

What if a draw-to-solve puzzle was about restoring something fragile?

Nature Seed began with a simple question: what if a draw-to-solve physics puzzle was not about pushing objects together, but about restoring something fragile?

We loved the clarity of simple line-drawing puzzle games, but wanted to create a calmer and more meaningful experience - one where every drawn line helps water reach a seed and every solved level brings a dying forest back to life.

Development history

From a small restoration idea to beta polish.

  • Summer 2025Original concept

    Nature Seed began as an idea for a calm mobile puzzle about connection, nature and restoration.

  • October 2025Seed and Water Drop identity

    The GDD was completed, and the Seed and Water Drop became the emotional center of the game's visual identity.

  • December 2025Development begins

    After the first paper levels were planned, active development started and the core systems moved into playable form.

  • February 2026Large draft level pass

    The game reached a large rough content pass, creating the foundation for later polish, balance and beta preparation.

  • March-April 2026Mechanics and level review

    Extra mechanics and draft levels were reviewed, simplified and reworked to keep the game calmer, clearer and more focused.

  • May-July 2026Public beta preparation

    The team is preparing the first public beta with focus on onboarding, restoration feedback, level clarity and mobile performance.
