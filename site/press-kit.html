Brand Guidelines

Willow W, portal mood and tactile motion.

  • Use the WillowinWorld mark as a clear first signal, not a tiny decorative signature.
  • Keep the brand palette balanced: moonlit cyan, fresh green, gold, rose and clean daylight surfaces.
  • Use the studio symbol as a portal/sigil cue, with the twin W mark and magical mobile worlds treated consistently.
  • Motion should feel like one small magical response: bloom, glint, drift or impact, not constant noise.
  • Preferred slogan lockup: Small games. Living worlds.
Our Worlds

Draw-to-Solve Physics Puzzle

Nature Seed

Playable Prototype Main genre: Draw-to-Solve Physics Puzzle

Nature Seed is a relaxing mobile draw-to-solve prototype about drawing limited lines that become physical objects. Its ten current levels ask players to bring Water Drop and Seed together, then improve the route for a better efficiency rating.

Press angle: a clear line-drawing hook, tactile physical routes and a soft restoration fantasy.

Casual Merge Puzzle

Candy Shop

Active Development Main genre: Casual Merge Puzzle

Candy Shop is a cozy mobile merge puzzle with a 13-stage candy chain, ten gameplay containers and eight special effect families. Players read a landing preview, plan the next-three queue, hold a candy and adapt to each container's rules.

Press angle: familiar drop-and-merge readability expanded through authored containers, planning tools and special effects.

Physics Puzzle / Arcade Hybrid

Paint Blasters

Production prototype Main genre: Physics Puzzle / Arcade Hybrid

Paint Blasters is a mobile color-destruction physics prototype with 35 implemented projectile recipes, seven block archetypes and a five-floor active tower. Players build the shot first, then aim for structural weak points and chain reactions.

Press angle: a colorful aim-and-shoot physics hook with a verified 35-recipe strategy layer.

Vertical Descent Arcade

Ball is God?!

Active Production Main genre: Story-driven Vertical Arcade

Ball is God?! is a hardcore vertical arcade where horizontal drag rotates a tower around auto-falling Espa. Skipping three platforms earns a combo smash, a cooldown shield can absorb a lethal hit, and Karma alters play across ten locations.

Press angle: a skill-based rotating-tower arcade with risk-reward movement, ten locations and story-sensitive Karma.

Asset Policy

Public files are preview-tier materials, not source packages.

  • Public files may be used for press articles, publisher review decks, store-reference mockups, creator coverage and WillowinWorld references.
  • Please do not crop, recolor, upscale or remove visible branding from public preview assets without asking first.
  • Layered files, original source art, unreleased screenshots, builds and non-public material are available only by direct request.
  • Credit WillowinWorld and link to the relevant game page or press kit when possible.

Full asset usage summary is available from the WillowinWorld footer as an on-page Asset Usage window.

Contact

Need source files, interviews, builds or unreleased material?

The public site only includes optimized preview assets. Original source files, unpublished builds, layered art, store packages and private materials are not stored in the public folder; request them directly with the project name and intended use.
