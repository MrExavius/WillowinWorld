Factsheet

Studio snapshot

WillowinWorld builds mobile-first games where readable mechanics, charming characters, tactile feedback and a strong visual brand work together. The portfolio currently spans draw-to-solve physics, candy merge puzzles, color destruction and vertical arcade descent.

Games

Current portfolio

Nature Seed

Relaxing draw-to-solve physics puzzle about drawing magical lines, guiding water to seeds and restoring a dying forest.

Candy Shop

Cozy candy merge puzzle with readable physics, special sweets, combos and long-term mobile progression.

Paint Blasters

Color destruction physics puzzle where players compose color-powered shots, smash towers and trigger chain reactions.

Ball is God?!

Hardcore vertical descent arcade about one-touch timing, divine towers, broken stones and story consequences.
