Privacy

WillowinWorld privacy note.

This website is a static public site for WillowinWorld games and press materials. It does not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels, third-party embeds, account systems or tracking cookies.

Contact email

If you email WillowinWorld, your message, email address and any links you provide are used only to reply to your inquiry.

Local theme

The site may store a small local theme preference in your browser so the day/night switch stays consistent.

Same-tab contact draft

While you fill in the homepage contact form, its name, email, inquiry type and message fields are kept in session storage so a reload does not erase your work. The draft stays on your device, can be cleared in the form and is removed when the browser tab session ends.

No third-party tracking

The public site avoids unnecessary third-party requests to reduce the amount of outside code loaded in your browser.
