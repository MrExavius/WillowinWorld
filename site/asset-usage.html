Asset Usage

Preview-tier public asset policy.

Public files on WillowinWorld game pages are optimized preview materials for press articles, publisher review decks, store-reference mockups and coverage about WillowinWorld games.

Allowed

Use public previews in articles, videos, social posts, publisher review decks and store-page mockups about WillowinWorld games, with WillowinWorld credit.

Ask first

Ask before cropping out branding, recoloring, using assets in paid ads, requesting layered source files or showing unreleased builds.

Not allowed

Do not resell, redistribute as asset packs, mint, use the assets to train AI or machine-learning datasets, impersonate the studio or use public assets for unrelated products.
