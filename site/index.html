Paint Blasters is a vibrant physics puzzle arcade about mixing colors and smashing blocks. Before every shot, players build a projectile from colors and charge types to unlock unique effects, combos and chain reactions. It blends precise aiming, tactical thinking, Match-3 logic and juicy arcade physics.

About WillowinWorld

A small studio building magical, tactile and emotionally memorable worlds.

WillowinWorld creates cozy but systemic games where every object feels touchable, every mechanic reads clearly and every character carries a little studio soul.

The magic cat is our living brand signal: curious, playful and quietly powerful. The twin W mark represents two connected worlds, with a hidden arc between them as the portal players cross when they enter our games.

We believe a world should not wait for a level to begin before it feels alive. Menus, cards, backgrounds and microinteractions can all carry atmosphere when they are designed with care.

Two W's, one portal.

Our mark is built like a constellation: two worlds held together by a hidden arc, a cat's spell and the promise that interaction can feel like magic.
