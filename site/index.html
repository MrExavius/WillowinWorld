BetaOptimization

Candy Shop is a cozy physics-based candy merge puzzle about dropping sweets, merging matching candies and building bigger treats through soft, satisfying physics. The game is now in internal beta, with optimization, UI polish, balance and combo readability as the current focus.

Paint Blasters

Production Draft Main genre: Physics Puzzle / Arcade Hybrid
DraftCore mechanics

Paint Blasters is a color destruction physics puzzle where players build color-powered projectiles before every shot, aim at readable towers and trigger chain reactions. The current GDD-based prototype has its core mechanics implemented in draft form while final scope is refined.

Studio Philosophy

Mechanic, art and emotion should work as one.

We design for instant readability, then deepen the system until every touch has weight, charm and feedback.

Playful Interaction

Every input deserves a reaction that feels intentional, readable and a little magical.

Readable Design

Systems should explain themselves through motion, contrast, pacing and clean player goals.

Character Identity

Mascots, objects and UI have personality before they have decoration.

Mobile-first UX

Controls, rhythm and layout are built for thumbs, short sessions and clear decisions.

Satisfying Feedback

Impact, merge, splash, win and fail moments are tuned until they feel crisp.

Cozy Depth

Cute can still be systemic, skillful and rewarding across many sessions.

2D Art Direction

Simple shapes, strong silhouettes and premium UI polish make worlds memorable.

Living Worlds

Backgrounds, UI and characters should quietly respond even outside the main loop.

About WillowinWorld

A small studio for tactile, magical mobile games.

WillowinWorld creates cozy but systemic games where every object feels touchable, every mechanic reads clearly and every character carries a little studio soul.

Mobile-first feel Cozy fantasy worlds Interactive systems Premium 2D art

The magic cat, twin W mark, living menus and playful microinteractions all support the same idea: the world should feel alive before a level even begins.

Two W's, one portal.

Our mark is built like a constellation: two worlds held together by a hidden arc, a cat's spell and the promise that interaction can feel like magic.

Process
