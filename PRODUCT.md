# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Players discovering distinctive mobile puzzle games, returning followers checking development progress, and publishers, press, or creators evaluating WillowinWorld and its four game worlds.

## Product Purpose

WillowinWorld is the studio hub and canonical discovery surface for Nature Seed, Candy Shop, Paint Blasters, and Ball is God. It should make each game understandable within seconds, reward deeper exploration with credible production detail, and turn interest into game-page visits, beta enquiries, press-kit use, and long-term studio recognition.

## Brand Personality

Magical, handcrafted, confident. The studio should feel like one imaginative universe containing four unmistakably different games: warm enough to invite curiosity, precise enough to earn publisher trust, and playful without becoming childish or noisy.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages, interchangeable AI card grids, and sterile corporate game-studio templates.
- A redesign that replaces or heavily mutes the existing magical layered background.
- A homepage composition that makes any of the four games look secondary; the main game gallery stays a clear, balanced 2×2 family on desktop.
- Re-skinning every game page into one visual theme or flattening its game-specific art direction.
- Unsupported roadmap promises, invented production claims, keyword stuffing, or visual spectacle that slows or obscures the content.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the world, strengthen the signal: keep the established background and art direction while making hierarchy, copy, and proof more decisive.
- Four games, four identities, one studio: share navigation and production quality without making the individual pages feel templated.
- Show real game truth: use shipped art and verified systems from the Unity projects instead of vague superlatives.
- Make the first screen cinematic and the next screens useful: every page should move naturally from premise to gameplay evidence, status, press assets, and a clear next action.
- Build trust through speed, accessibility, and honest status language; polish must never come at the cost of readability or mobile performance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across keyboard access, focus visibility, contrast, semantic structure, touch targets, responsive reflow, and reduced-motion support. Decorative motion must pause or simplify when requested, while essential game information remains available as text and standard HTML images.
